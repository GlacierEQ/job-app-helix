Public access does not make the materials open source and does not grant production, modification, redistribution, model-training, derivative-work, or commercial-use rights.

## What Reviewers May Do

A bona fide recruiter, hiring team, partner, investor, customer, researcher, or licensing prospect may inspect the materials internally to evaluate Casey Barton / GlacierEQ for:

- employment;
- partnership;
- procurement or customer deployment;
- commercial or non-commercial licensing;
- systems integration;
- joint development;
- investment or acquisition;
- research collaboration;
- a separately negotiated source-available or open-source arrangement.

## What Requires a Written Agreement

- production or hosted deployment;
- copying or redistributing substantial source or documentation;
- modification or derivative works;
- commercial use;
- external benchmarking publication;
- training or fine-tuning models on the materials;
- use of GlacierEQ names, marks, or product identities;
- access to non-public supporting materials.

## Growth Position

The proprietary posture protects specialized systems and methods while keeping the door open to the right employment, partnership, customer, research, investment, licensing, or open-source conversation. Terms can be tailored to the opportunity through a separate written agreement.
