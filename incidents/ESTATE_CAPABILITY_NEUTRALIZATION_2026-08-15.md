# Estate Capability Neutralization Incident — 2026-08-15

Status: **ACTIVE RECOVERY**

The estate has entered recovery mode for a systemic failure pattern: repository repair/governance work repeatedly converted unfinished or ambitious capability into bounded local, synthetic, report-only, receipt-only, proposal-only, or proof-only surfaces. Verification controls became product ceilings.

## Governing correction

The approved product capability is the unit of completion. Governance, proof, security, provenance, and public-truth layers exist to make capability trustworthy; they do not replace capability.

**Ambition is not a defect to optimize away.** GlacierEQ systems are intentionally oversized, compositional, and forward-reaching. When the destination exceeds one execution cycle, the response is not to minimize the destination, the architecture, the mechanism, the integration surface, the verification target, or the recovery scope.

The execution law is **MAXIMUM COHERENT ADVANCE**: take the largest coherent, executable, testable, reversible, authority-valid tranche the available system and tool surface can support while preserving every prior gain. If multiple independent fronts can advance safely, advance them concurrently or in the same execution wave. Do not introduce artificial serialization or a preference for tiny changes merely because they are easier to reason about.

When a public claim outruns implementation, the default engineering response is:

1. preserve the implementation lineage and experimental mechanisms;
2. preserve the full intended architecture and north-star capability;
3. mark only the unsupported projection as unproven;
4. build as much of the missing implementation as can be coherently completed in the current execution tranche;
5. compose that implementation with every compatible existing capability rather than isolating it into a smaller product;
6. retain later bug/security/provenance improvements;
7. verify the complete changed capability tranche at exact current head;
8. continue immediately into the next power-increasing tranche while authorization and executable work remain.

The default response is **not** to redefine the repository downward until its tests are easy to satisfy, and it is **not** to choose the smallest possible implementation merely because a smaller change is easier to verify.

## Anti-minimization law

Minimization is prohibited as a default optimization at every layer:

- **Objective:** do not replace the requested destination with a safer, weaker, narrower, conventional, or easier objective.
- **Architecture:** do not collapse a distributed/compositional system into a toy local surface just to reduce complexity.
- **Implementation:** do not stop at an MVP, stub, scaffold, thin slice, or one-path demonstration when a larger coherent mechanism can be built now.
- **Integration:** do not leave working components disconnected when the current authority and interfaces allow composition.
- **Execution:** do not serialize independent work fronts merely to make the plan look tidy.
- **Proof:** do not shrink the product to match the proof harness; expand the harness to prove the product tranche that was actually built.
- **Recovery:** do not restore one tiny regression while leaving adjacent recoverable capabilities amputated if they can be restored coherently in the same wave.
- **Completion:** do not create an artificial bus stop after a green gate when the next authorized power increase is executable.

Safety, provenance, reversibility, exact-head identity, and authority remain hard constraints. They constrain **how power is added**, not how little power the system is allowed to have.

## Recovery rule

Every active canonical repository must be compared against its strongest legitimate prior capability state. Any removed/disabled path must be classified as:

- unsupported claim only;
- duplicate/superseded mechanism;
- unsafe unbounded path requiring a controlled authority gate; or
- real capability regression.

Real capability regressions are restored or completed on fresh canonical ancestry. Old source bytes and provenance remain preserved. A repository is not recovered until its canonical capability is at least as strong as the strongest legitimate prior state, while keeping later correctness and security gains. Recovery should operate on the **largest coherent capability family** that can be safely reconstructed and verified, not on an arbitrarily minimized unit.
