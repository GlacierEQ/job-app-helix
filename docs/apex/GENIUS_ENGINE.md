# Genius Engine

**Module:** `job_app_helix.genius_engine`  
**CLI:** `python scripts/genius_engine.py`  
**Identity:** APEX is the counter to canonical destruction  
**Law:** MAXIMUM_COHERENT_ADVANCE

## Purpose

Turn each worthy repository or company track into the strongest truthful version of itself by **inventing profound bottleneck-removing mechanisms** — not wrappers, not stamps, not MVP amputations.

## Scoring

- **novelty** — mechanism specificity, non-wrapper language, domain fit  
- **coherence** — complete fields, dual-plane / MCA alignment, anti-neutralization fitness  
- **genius** — `0.45 * novelty + 0.55 * coherence`  
- Adversarial gate rejects incomplete, theatrical, or neutralization-pattern solutions
