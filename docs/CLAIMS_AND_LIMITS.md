# Claims and Limits

## What this repository proves

- A clean Python package can model a build, verify, refine, and decide loop.
- Every hold or warning can carry stable, human-readable evidence.
- A recovery can be restricted to contingency information declared before reassessment.
- A final campaign decision can remain fail-closed and machine-readable.
- The public repository can enforce its own navigation and disclosure boundary in CI.

## What this repository does not prove

- Production deployment in aerospace, datacenter, vehicle, medical, legal, or other regulated environments.
- Manufacturer-approved thresholds or operational procedures.
- Employment, endorsement, or affiliation with companies whose domains inspired portfolio work.
- That every repository in the wider GlacierEQ account has equal depth or production readiness.
- That a passing demonstration substitutes for domain review, validation data, certification, or safety analysis.

## Demonstration thresholds

The numerical envelopes in `pistons.py` are deterministic portfolio fixtures. They exist to demonstrate control flow, evidence handling, and fail-closed behavior. They must not be presented as manufacturer specifications.

## Disclosure policy

This hiring surface excludes legal and family-case projects, secrets, generated IDE memory, private state, personal machine paths, and unpublished readiness claims. The CI public-surface check enforces that boundary for tracked files.
