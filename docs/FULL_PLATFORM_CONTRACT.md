# GlacierEQ Full-Platform Contract

## Coherent system purpose

The GlacierEQ Hiring System is one coherent public hiring platform. It connects recruiter, résumé, master, mesh, atlas, and machine surfaces with Job-App Helix authority, repository-native evidence, operational services, typed APIs, data, artifacts, integrations, automation, observability, security controls, delivery verification, and recovery.

## Authority and platform law

Job-App Helix is the build-time portfolio authority. It preserves the factual graph, repository-native provenance, and public-safe evidence projections that the hiring system consumes. Connected repositories retain authority over their own code, tests, deployment evidence, and operational state. The public hiring platform renders and operates from these connected contracts without impersonating or overwriting their authority.

## Extensible platform law

The system evolves as a connected platform rather than as an isolated presentation surface. Its frontend, typed backend APIs, authenticated operations, authorization, normalized data, artifact storage, integrations, automation, observability, security controls, CI/CD, deployment verification, and recovery pathways are designed to develop together through explicit contracts.

## Evidence and growth law

Evidence boundaries govern the claims attached to a given artifact, behavior, integration, deployment, or outcome. They do not impose arbitrary caps on systems, repositories, projects, capabilities, contributors, or future platform growth. Audience-specific views remain projections of the wider capability graph; no projection defines the whole estate.

## Delivery law

The public and operational layers evolve through compatible, additive change that preserves valid gains. Every material change is traceable through source authority, typed contracts, implementation, verification, delivery evidence, observability, and recovery readiness. The platform is truthful about current states while remaining open to further discovery, composition, extension, and deployment.
