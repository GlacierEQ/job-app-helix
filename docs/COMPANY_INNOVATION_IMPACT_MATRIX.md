## Fleet interpretation

- **Highest compounding lane:** semantic/intent systems — they can connect repository-specific mechanisms to shared evidence, memory, retrieval, and orchestration.
- **Highest failure-cost lane:** authority, mission, safety, and resource-control systems — their upside is large, but they should compound through adversarial tests, recovery, and evidence-bound actuation.
- **Highest infrastructure leverage:** placement, energy, admission, SLO, and spend mechanisms — telemetry-driven closed-loop optimization is the next major capability jump.
- **Portfolio effect:** a common impact vector now lets Spiral/Tower-style iteration choose *where the next unit of engineering effort creates the largest coherent gain* without erasing company specialization.
