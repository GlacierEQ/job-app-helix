## Preserved deliberately

- Historical incident reports, status receipts, and solidification records: they are provenance, not active instructions.
- `EVIDENCE_BOUND`, `least privilege`, refusal states, controlled retries, resource-aware pools, and explicit boundaries: these are behavior and safety contracts, not ambition limits.
- Schema and compatibility semantics were changed only where all active producers, consumers, and tests were migrated together. Runtime safety bounds remain explicit; ambition ceilings do not.
